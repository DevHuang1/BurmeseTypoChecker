# Reviewed Domain Lexicon Sources

The domain lexicons in this prototype are deliberately limited to reviewable term sets. They should be expanded through a documented editorial workflow rather than by treating an unverified web list as a complete dictionary.

| Domain | Reference | Review use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal | [English-Myanmar Law Dictionary](https://books.google.com/books/about/English_Myanmar_Law_Dictionary.html?id=WsKFtgAACAAJ), 3rd edition, 303 pages, published by the Attorney General’s Office in 2002 | Confirms the availability of a dedicated English–Myanmar legal dictionary and supplies the legal-domain scope for the curated terms. |
| Medical | [Medical Terms – English, Karen & Burmese](https://www.burmalibrary.org/en/medical-terms-english-karen-burmese), Drum Publications, 2008 | Confirms a 32-page multilingual medical terminology list intended for consultation contexts and supplies the medical-domain scope for the curated terms. |

The terms committed in the application are small, manually reviewed collections for product behavior and regression testing. They are not represented as a complete legal or medical dictionary.
